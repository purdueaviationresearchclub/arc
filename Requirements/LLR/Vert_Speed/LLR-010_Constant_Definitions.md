# L2-001: Constant Definitions

## Statement
Specific gas constant for air (R) shall be defined as 1,716 ft*lbf/slug*R˚.

Sea Level Temperature (T0) shall be defined as 518.67 degrees R˚.

Standard Sea Level Density (ρ) shall be defined as 0.002377 slug/ft^3.

Standard Acceleration of Gravity (g) shall be defined as 32.174 ft/s^2.
